## Design system (already copied into src/app/design-system/)

### Hard rules (from SKILL.md — violate none)
- Ink `#081019` is text. Baby Blue `#E3EDF5` is primary bg (~60%). White ~25%. Misty Blue `#ACC4D6` secondary/borders ~10%. Electric Cobalt `#0052FF` accent/links/CTA ~5%.
- **Cobalt is NEVER text or button-fill on Ink (dark).** On dark sections, cobalt appears only as fill behind white/Baby Blue type. One cobalt CTA per view.
- **Josefin Sans** for headings ONLY (never running text). **Sen** for everything else INCLUDING all numerals.
- Headings track negative (-0.005 to -0.02em). Uppercase Josefin +0.12em+ tracking. Body tracking 0.
- **border-radius: 2px EVERYWHERE.** No pills, no soft cards.
- **No shadows, no gradients, no glows, no photographic imagery, no illustration.** Flat color fields only. Borders (1px Misty Blue) do separation.
- Buttons: solid fill only — no outline/ghost/text-only. Primary = Cobalt fill/white label (light) or Baby Blue fill/Ink label (dark). Secondary = Misty Blue fill/Ink label, never on plain white.
- Line length caps at 75ch.

### Voice (mechanism-then-payoff, numbers not adjectives)
- Every technical claim followed by what it means for the reader.
- Banned words: revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, military-grade, unlock, empower, game-changing, anonymous (as headline), untraceable, mixer, shielded.
- Use: private, auditable, provable, settlement, operator/integrator, compliance path, specified/unspecified.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji. Lead with the claim, no throat-clearing.
